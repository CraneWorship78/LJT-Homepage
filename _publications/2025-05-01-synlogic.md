**Authors:** Junteng Liu\*, Yuanxiang Fan\*, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, Junxian He

**Abstract:** Synthesizing high-quality reasoning data has emerged as a scalable paradigm for enhancing the logical reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs). In this paper, we introduce SynLogic, a framework for Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond. SynLogic leverages symbolic reasoning and verifiers to automatically construct reasoning trajectories with objective correctness signals, enabling the generation of large-scale, high-quality reasoning datasets without human annotation. We apply SynLogic to synthetic logical reasoning domains and demonstrate that models trained on SynLogic-generated data achieve substantial improvements on multiple logical reasoning benchmarks, outperforming models trained on human-annotated or heuristically-generated reasoning data. Further, we show that the reasoning skills acquired through SynLogic transfer beneficially to out-of-domain reasoning tasks, indicating the generality of the synthesized data.
