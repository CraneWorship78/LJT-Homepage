**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Shiqi Chen, Yu Cheng, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We investigate whether a universal representation of truthfulness exists inside Large Language Models. By probing the internal representations of LLMs, we discover a universal truthfulness hyperplane — a directionally consistent structure that encodes the truthfulness of model predictions across diverse tasks. We demonstrate that this hyperplane generalizes across models, datasets, and tasks, and we show its utility for spotting and mitigating untruthful outputs. Our findings suggest that truthfulness is not an emergent behavior alone, but is deeply structured within the representations of LLMs, offering a unified means to assess and steer model honesty.
