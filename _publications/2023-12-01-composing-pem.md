**Authors:** Jinghan Zhang, Shiqi Chen, Junteng Liu, Junxian He

**Abstract:** Parameter-Efficient Modules (PEMs) such as LoRA adapters enable lightweight task customization of large pre-trained models. We investigate whether multiple PEMs can be composed via arithmetic operations (e.g., addition, scalarization) to produce new modules that transfer compositionally to new tasks and domains. Empirically, we show that arithmetic compositions of PEMs are viable, producing modules that retain the benefits of each individual PEM and sometimes outperform them. Our results suggest a modular paradigm for model adaptation, where a growing collection of task-specific PEMs may be combined on-the-fly to address new tasks without additional training.
