**Authors:** Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He

**Abstract:** Chart understanding is a critical multimodal reasoning capability that requires Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to accurately perceive and reason over visual and textual elements in charts. In this work, we investigate the perception bottleneck of VLMs for chart understanding, systematically analyzing where and how VLMs fail when processing visual inputs from charts. Through extensive experiments and ablation studies, we identify specific failure modes in low-level visual perception that propagate to downstream reasoning errors, and we propose targeted interventions to improve chart understanding performance. Our findings provide insights for designing more robust VLM architectures and training pipelines for chart-related tasks.
