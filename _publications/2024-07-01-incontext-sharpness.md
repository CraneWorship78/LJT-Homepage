**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

**Abstract:** Hallucination remains a central challenge for Large Language Models (LLMs), where models generate fluent but factually incorrect outputs. In this work, we propose to use in-context sharpness of inner representations as an alert signal for hallucination mitigation. We observe that the sharpness of hidden representations in the in-context samples correlates with the factuality of the model's generation, and leverage this signal to detect and reduce hallucinations. Our approach provides a scalable, training-free means to improve the faithfulness of LLM outputs across multiple tasks without modifying model weights.
