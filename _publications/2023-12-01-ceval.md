**Authors:** Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, Junxian He

**Abstract:** We introduce C-Eval, a comprehensive multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. C-Eval consists of 1,394 multi-choice questions across four levels of difficulty and 52 distinct disciplines, covering a broad range of knowledge and skills required by Chinese learners and professionals. The suite is designed to assess the knowledge, reasoning, and comprehension capabilities of foundation models in Chinese. We benchmark a wide range of popular LLMs on C-Eval and provide detailed analyses of model performance across different categories and difficulty levels, offering insights into the strengths and weaknesses of current models in Chinese-language understanding and reasoning.
